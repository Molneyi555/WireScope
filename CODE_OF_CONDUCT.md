# WireScope community code of conduct

## Our commitment

WireScope welcomes people of every background and experience level. Maintainers and contributors commit to making project spaces safe, respectful, technically curious, and focused on constructive collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and specific when discussing code or ideas.
- Critique the work, never the person doing the work.
- Respect different levels of experience and explain unfamiliar concepts when practical.
- Protect private network data, credentials, and identifying information.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help repair harm.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention in project spaces.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing unredacted captures, credentials, private URLs, or other sensitive artifacts.
- Encouraging unauthorized interception, surveillance, or network access.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, community calls, and other spaces where someone represents WireScope.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary disagreements, ask a maintainer to moderate the thread. For a private or sensitive report, use the repository's [private advisory form](https://github.com/Molneyi555/WireScope/security/advisories/new) and mark the title as a code-of-conduct report.

Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to review the report, consider context and impact, and respond proportionally. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary participation limit, content removal, or permanent exclusion. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is not tolerated.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and adapted to WireScope's privacy and authorized-use requirements.
